A nationally recognized full-service restaurant brand needed a unified training and operations system that could work everywhere — including locations without reliable internet — while staying on-brand and accessible to every level of staff.

The Solution

Three custom HTML platforms built with AI, designed to run completely offline from a thumb drive. No IT infrastructure. No monthly fees. No training required to use them.
