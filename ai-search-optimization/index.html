What is AEO, in plain English?

More customers now ask an AI assistant who to call instead of scrolling through search results. The assistant answers with a few business names. AEO is the work of making sure yours can be one of them: AI crawlers can reach your site, your business details are stated clearly and consistently, and your pages answer the questions customers actually ask.

AEO builds on regular SEO. Search engines still matter, and many AI answers draw on search results, so a site that is strong for Google is usually a good start for AI too.

What we check and fix.

  • AI crawler access: robots.txt rules for 12 crawlers, including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and Bingbot.
  • Firewall and CDN blocks: "block AI bots" settings that silently override robots.txt.
  • Readable HTML: real text in the page, not content that only appears after JavaScript runs.
  • Business schema: Organization or LocalBusiness data with your name, location, phone and service area.
  • Answer-ready content: a clear opening answer and a visible FAQ with matching FAQPage schema.
  • Consistent business info: the same name, phone and area on your site, Google Business Profile, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect.

AI search optimization questions.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is making your business easy for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini to find, read, trust and cite. It is the AI-era extension of SEO.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in search results pages. AEO focuses on being named and quoted inside AI-generated answers. They overlap heavily: crawlable pages, clear business details and helpful content serve both.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Nobody can guarantee AI recommendations, because AI answers change from run to run. Floorline AI removes the technical barriers, makes your business information clear and consistent, and measures your AI mentions over time.

How long does it take to show up in AI answers?

Technical fixes take effect as soon as AI crawlers revisit your site, but each engine re-crawls on its own schedule, so changes in AI answers usually take weeks to months. Monthly monitoring shows you the trend.

Do I need a new website for AEO?

Usually not. Most fixes, such as robots.txt rules, schema and FAQ content, can be added to your existing site. Some website builders limit what can be changed, and we'll tell you up front if yours does.

What is llms.txt, and do I need it?

llms.txt is a proposed plain-text file that summarizes a site for AI tools. No major AI engine has confirmed using it yet, so we treat it as optional. It's cheap to add, but it isn't scored in our audit.
